    What do these numbers mean?
    Gross production
    The full fee value of all completed dental work, before insurance write-offs.
    Collection rate
    Of what patients and insurers owe after write-offs, the share actually collected. Healthy offices stay above 95%.
    Staff labor %
    Hourly staff pay (from UKG) as a share of production. Lower means the team is efficient for the work being done.
    Cost per new patient
    Advertising spend divided by new patients seen. It shows what it costs to bring someone in.
    No-show rate
    Share of scheduled appointments where the patient didn't come.
    Operating margin
    What's left of collections after every operating expense: payroll, doctors, rent, supplies, repairs, software and more.
